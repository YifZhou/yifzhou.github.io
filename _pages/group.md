  <section class="people-section" id="prospective-students" aria-labelledby="prospective-students-title">
    <h2 id="prospective-students-title">Prospective students</h2>
    <div class="summary-grid summary-grid--wide">
      <article class="summary-card">
        <h3>Science</h3>
        <p>Our projects address gas accretion onto young planets, clouds and chemistry in atmospheres, and methods for observing faint companions. The research pages and Deep Dive articles describe recent examples.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="summary-card">
        <h3>Preparation</h3>
        <p>Physics, astronomy, programming, and statistics are useful preparation. Prior experience with JWST is not required. Students learn the observing and analysis methods as part of their research.</p>
      </article>
      <article class="summary-card">
        <h3>Get in touch</h3>
        <p>I welcome inquiries from prospective students. Please <a href="mailto:{{ site.author.email }}">email me</a> about your research interests, background, and when you hope to begin. A CV or short summary of your experience is helpful.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
