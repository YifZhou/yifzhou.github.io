    <p class="eyebrow">Teaching and mentoring</p>
    <h1>Teaching and mentoring</h1>
    <p class="lead">I teach astronomy through physical questions and the observations that help answer them. Students learn to interpret data, test ideas, and explain their reasoning.</p>
  </header>

  <section class="teaching-grid teaching-grid--wide" aria-label="Teaching overview">
    <article class="teaching-card teaching-card--wide">
      <p class="card-kicker">ASTR 1250</p>
      <h2>Alien Worlds</h2>
      <p>An introduction to planets beyond the Solar System. Students learn how observations reveal the sizes, orbits, atmospheres, and histories of other worlds.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="teaching-card teaching-card--wide">
      <p class="card-kicker">ASTR 5110</p>
      <h2>Astronomical Techniques</h2>
      <p>How astronomical instruments work and how we analyze their data. Students connect observing methods, measurement uncertainties, and physical interpretation.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="teaching-card teaching-card--wide">
      <p class="card-kicker">ASTR 1610</p>
      <h2>Introduction to Astronomical Research</h2>
      <p>Guest lectures introduce astronomical research questions and the observing and analysis methods used to address them.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="teaching-card teaching-card--wide">
      <p class="card-kicker">Mentoring</p>
      <h2>Research with a team</h2>
      <p>Students develop a research question, learn the relevant analysis methods, and discuss their results regularly. I encourage them to test their assumptions and take increasing responsibility for the project.</p>
    </article>
  </section>

  <section class="split-band" aria-labelledby="teaching-philosophy">
    <div>
      <p class="eyebrow">Teaching philosophy</p>
      <h2 id="teaching-philosophy">Connecting observations to physics</h2>
      <p>I emphasize physical understanding, quantitative reasoning, and clear communication. Students should be able to explain why a measurement supports an interpretation and how they would test it further.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="split-band__aside">
      <p>I treat uncertainty as part of scientific reasoning. Comparing alternative explanations helps students identify what they can conclude and what they still need to measure.</p>
    </div>
  </section>
